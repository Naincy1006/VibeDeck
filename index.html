About VibeDeck

Suggestions shaped by your vibe.

The Concept

Traditional streaming platforms sort content by rigid, clinical genres like Action, Comedy, or Drama. But human emotions don't always fit into neat little boxes. Sometimes you aren't looking for generic "Drama"-you're looking for something deeply Thought-Provoking, a dose of Motivational energy, or a heavy Dark-Intense thriller. VibeDeck bridges that gap by mapping curation to how you actually feel.

How It Works

By tagging every single title with multi-layered emotional contexts, VibeDeck allows you to filter through entire collections dynamically. Whether you select a single mood or look for intersecting sub-genres, the deck re-shuffles instantly to serve your current state of mind.

Behind the Deck

VibeDeck is an independent frontend project built to experiment with dynamic DOM manipulation, asynchronous UI filtering, and custom layout architectures.

Tech Stack: HTML5 • CSS3 • JavaScript (ES6)
