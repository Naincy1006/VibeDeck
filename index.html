The Concept

Traditional streaming platforms sort content by rigid, standard genres like Action, Comedy, or Drama. But sometimes the human emotions don't just belong to one specific genre.We bridge that gap between your emotions and the stories available by curating them according to much narrower categories.

How It Works

By tagging every single title with multi-layered emotional contexts, VibeDeck allows you to filter through entire collections dynamically. Whether you select a single mood or look for intersecting sub-genres, the deck re-shuffles instantly to serve your current state of mind.

Behind the Deck

VibeDeck is an independent frontend project built to experiment with dynamic DOM manipulation, asynchronous UI filtering, and custom layout architectures.

Tech Stack: HTML5 • CSS3 • JavaScript (ES6)
